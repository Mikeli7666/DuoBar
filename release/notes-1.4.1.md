## What's new in 1.4.1

- **Desktop Widget** — DuoBar now lives on your desktop too: the same 3-in-1 glyph, draggable anywhere, in three sizes. Turn it on in Settings.
- **Language switching** — follow the system language or pick your own: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Русский, Українська, ไทย.
- **Popover, refined** — on macOS 26 the popover glass now matches the native Control Center look, and the popover opens and closes instantly.
- **Settings window** can now be resized and minimized.
- Option-click the network row in the popover to jump straight to Network settings.

## Fixes

- Battery percentage no longer breaks at the Extra Large icon size (thanks to @lnobre on Threads for the report).
