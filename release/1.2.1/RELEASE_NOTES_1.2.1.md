# DuoBar 1.2.1

DuoBar 1.2.1 is a focused compatibility, reliability, and community update.

## What's New

- Improved Wi-Fi network-name reliability and Location permission support
- More compact Wi-Fi control
- Switch audio output devices directly from the DuoBar popover
- Quick access to Network, Battery, and Sound settings
- Russian and Ukrainian localization
- Build and compatibility/reliability work with Xcode 27 and the macOS 27 SDK, while continuing to support macOS 13+

## Distribution

- Apple Developer ID signed
- Apple notarized
- macOS 13 or later
- Apple Silicon and Intel
- Universal 2
- Free and open source
